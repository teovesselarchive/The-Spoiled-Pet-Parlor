# Research and design translation

Reviewed November 12, 2026. References inform an original concept; no measured conversion lift is claimed.

## The owner's visual references

- [Grüns](https://gruns.co/): visually inspected current homepage and promotion presentation. Bold friendly typography, uncluttered choices, product-led photography and repeated clear calls to action. Adapted the confidence and approachable language; did not copy nutrition claims, review counts or discount mechanics.
- [Bloom Nutrition](https://bloomnu.com/): visually inspected current homepage. Saturated campaign imagery, pastel product worlds, rounded actions and a prominent quiz. Adapted that joyful product-photography language for pet portraits and a service finder.

## Grooming patterns

- [Barkbus](https://www.barkbus.com/): visually inspected homepage; clear booking path, phone/text access, service-area check, included-services information and FAQs. Adapted booking clarity and first-visit reassurance; no mobile-service claim was imported.
- [Scenthound](https://www.scenthound.com/): core care package, optional services and plans. Informed simple service differentiation. Memberships were omitted until the business confirms them.
- [Pawradise](https://www.pawradise.com/): call/text/book paths, service links, before/after photographs and FAQs. Informed easy contact and an interactive lookbook; simulated before/after outcomes were deliberately avoided.
- [Pawmper Fluffy](https://pawmperfluffy.com/services/): bath versus haircut packages and size/coat categories. Informed the short goal-and-size finder and call-for-quote approach.
