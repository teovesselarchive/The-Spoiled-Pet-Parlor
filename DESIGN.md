# A little fluff. A lot of fabulous.

An original pet beauty-club identity: Fredoka's soft, expressive lettering, Nunito Sans for readable details, raspberry actions, creamy ground, blush surfaces and photographic campaign portraits. This follows the owner's brief for Grüns/Bloom-like fun, bubbly, feminine energy without copying their logos, code, product imagery or wording.

## Tokens

- Cream #fff9ee; deep berry text #582635; raspberry accent #a72154
- Pink #f8cddd; pale #fff0f5; supporting text #6f4651
- Full-pill actions; 10px inputs; 20–24px interactive panels; a deliberately arched hero photograph
- Display: Fredoka 600; body: Nunito Sans 400/700/800, self-hosted
- Phone layout composes the headline and portrait vertically. CTA links stay visible in a mobile bottom bar.

## Composition

A spacious two-column hero opens with a large pet portrait and the message. A brand ribbon leads into a photographic menu. The three-question finder sits on a pink field, followed by a playful portrait lookbook, an inline appointment wish-list planner, FAQs and a closing brand moment. Real reviews, staff biographies and price claims are absent because they have not been supplied.

## Interaction

One short entrance animation; press and hover feedback; a tap-triggered virtual boop. No looping carousel or scroll hijack. Reduced motion removes animation and smooth scrolling. The quiz transfers service, size and personality into the planner. All planning happens locally and ends in an honest summary plus the supplied phone number.

## Evidence and limitations

Only the business name and number are confirmed. Menu inclusions are explicitly illustrative. Pet photography is AI-created concept campaign work, not evidence of customers or grooming outcomes. No before/after pairs or testimonials are fabricated. Reference sites and awards are recorded in RESEARCH.md. The Elite Dance Floors design record was reviewed for process and interaction quality; its visual identity was not reused.

The installed design context engine could not run in this environment. No output from that engine is claimed; direction follows the explicit brief and inspected references.
